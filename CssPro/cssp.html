About Me

I am a third-year Computer Science Student focused on web development and software engineering with a passion for creating innovative solutions and learning new technologies.

How did a village boy end up writing code?

Where I grew up, the nearest thing to a computer was a rumour. I had never touched the internet, but I was already curious about it, a world I could not see, built by people I had never met. That curiosity never went away. It followed me out of the village and into a Computer Science classroom, where the rumour finally became real.

Today I build websites for real clients, which still feels a little unreal. The boy who wondered how computers worked is now the one making them work for others, and he is nowhere near done learning.
